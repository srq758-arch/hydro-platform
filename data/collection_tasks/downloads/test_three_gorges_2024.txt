Three Gorges Dam 2024 Generation Report
========================================

Station: Three Gorges Dam (三峡大坝)
Location: Yichang, Hubei Province, China
Operator: China Three Gorges Corporation

2024 Annual Generation Summary:
- Total Generation: 103.0 TWh (103,000 GWh)
- Capacity Factor: 52.1%
- Operating Hours: 8,760 hours
- Units Online: 34 units (32 main + 2 underground)

Historical Context:
By August 2025, the Three Gorges Dam has generated a cumulative total of 1.8 trillion kWh
since beginning operations in 2003.
